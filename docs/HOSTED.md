# A hosted GitHub App (not built)

This page is a statement of intent, not a product. Nothing here exists yet.

**What stays open and free.** The CLI, the GitHub Action, the routing rules, the benchmark and its data, under the MIT license. The Action runs on your own `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; the CLI runs on your own Claude plan or key. Nothing in the open-source tool phones home.

**What a hosted app could add** (and might be paid): installing once for an organization instead of one workflow per repo, review of fork PRs without sharing secrets, per-repo budgets and a spend dashboard, a shared `.tierview.yml` across repos, and receipts kept over time so a team can see what its reviews cost.

**What it would not change.** Findings and receipts would stay in the same format, the router would stay deterministic and inspectable, and every price shown would still be the API-equivalent cost at list prices with its date.

**Whether to build it** depends on whether people use the open-source version. If you would use it, or would not, open an issue and say why.
