# Benchmark cases

30 cases: 20 bug-introducing commits whose later fix names them (maintainer-attested), 10 presumed-clean commits. Dev split 10 (the 5 pilot cases are marked P), test split 20. Generated by `bench/validate.ts make bench/selection.json`; the selection and one-sentence bug descriptions were written by hand in the Phase 4 manual pass. Each `<id>.json` has the metadata, fix and faulty lines; `<id>.diff` is exactly what the reviewer sees.

All fixes were committed between 2026-08-23 and 2026-11-13, after mid-2026 (PLAN §9 contamination rule); the bug commits themselves are older (2011 to 2026). Clean commits are from 2025-08 to 2025-11, so 12 months of later history could be checked for fixes.

## How cases were chosen

- Mined from fix commits after 2026-07-01 (`bench/sources.json`): `Fixes: <sha>` trailers (QEMU, DPDK, Cilium, Tetragon, cloud-hypervisor), Django's "Regression in <sha>", "introduced in <sha>" (cloud-hypervisor, Tailscale), and "regression from #PR" resolved to the PR's merge commit (Next.js). 531 links in `bench/candidates.jsonl`.
- Automatic checks (`bench/validated.jsonl`): both commits exist, neither is a merge, B is an ancestor of F, B ≤ 400 lines and ≤ 15 files, F ≤ 60 lines, B mostly in the case's language, and `git blame B^..F^` attributes at least one line F removed or rewrote to B. Faulty lines are those blamed lines, in B's numbering.
- Manual pass dropped a test-only flakiness fix (Next.js), a hardening change that is not a bug (DPDK hash seed), and a cleanup (QEMU sev helper); kept one fix per bug commit; capped each repo at 6 cases.
- Clean commits (`bench/clean.jsonl`): non-merge, 10 to 400 lines, mostly the language, not tests only, no later commit message names them, and no line rewritten by a fix-labelled commit within 12 months. Commits whose files saw more than 10 fix-labelled commits in that year were skipped (to bound blame time), which biases the clean set toward calmer code. Dropped by hand: a translation-only commit, a header-only commit and a TypeScript version bump.
- Known skew: C has 10 of 20 buggy cases and TypeScript only 1. Squash-merge TypeScript repos rarely name the PR that caused a bug, and most of their fixes were over 60 lines (Angular) or the link pointed at the fix's own PR (MUI).
